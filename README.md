# combining-models

A short comment (about 1,500 words) on the validity of multi-model combinations in outbreak modelling collaborations.

Developed from the analytic commentary of Kath Sherratt's PhD by publication (LSHTM, defended June 2024), chapter "Evaluating collaborative modelling and multi-model combination", updated with work from 2024 to 2026.
